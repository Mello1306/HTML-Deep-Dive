Arts and Crafts: The Precious Time

I am into arts and crafts, which is basically a fancy way of saying I have a black belt in hot gluing and an ever-growing collection of supplies I'll definitely use... eventually.

The Reset Button

Jokes apart, arts and crafts is more than just a way to pass time for me; it's a way to hit the reset button on the world.

The Creative Process

It's the one space where you get to be the architect of your own ideas, turning raw materials like paper, paint, or thread into something tangible that didn't exist an hour ago.

Finding the Flow State

There's a specific kind of magic in the flow state you find when you're focused on the tiny details of a project; it's meditative, a little bit messy, and incredibly rewarding.

-Vaibhavi Singh
